My Journey

From Computing & Creative Design to Cybersecurity

My journey began with childhood curiosity about how systems work and break, evolved through software engineering, brand design, and video production, and now converges into Cybersecurity — bringing a rare multidisciplinary perspective to modern digital defense.

Stage 01 • Education London Metropolitan University

Building a Foundation in Computing & Networking

I graduated from London Metropolitan University with a Bachelor's degree in Computing, developing strong foundations in software development, programming, problem-solving, databases, and digital technologies.

My fascination with security actually started in childhood — since Grade 8, I was deeply curious about cybersecurity, exploring basic security concepts, ethical hacking, phishing mechanics, and how cyber attacks operate behind the scenes.

During my Bachelor's degree, I gravitated strongly toward Networking modules across both Level 4 and Level 5, achieving advanced knowledge in network architecture, protocols, and infrastructure. During summer breaks, I also studied CCNA and Cisco networking fundamentals, cementing practical routing, switching, and network security concepts.

BSc (Hons) Computing Level 4 & 5 Networking CCNA & Cisco Fundamentals Security Curiosity Since Grade 8
Stage 02 • Web Development 300+ Real Bookings Processed

Turning Ideas into Functional & Resilient Experiences

I began developing responsive websites and full-stack web applications using technologies including Python, Django, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. Working on real-world projects taught me how thoughtful system architecture turns an idea into a reliable, high-utility digital product.

One of my flagship projects was engineering and deploying a fully responsive grooming booking platform for Little Heart Pet Shop. The platform unified clean frontend UI, Django backend logic, MySQL database management, and automated booking workflows — successfully processing 300+ real customer bookings.

Python & Django MySQL Database JavaScript & Bootstrap

Stage 06 • Present Day & Next Chapter Active Career Transition

Transitioning into Cybersecurity with a Hybrid Perspective

Currently, I am actively pursuing a specialized Cybersecurity course while leveraging my real-world professional experience across web development, visual design, and video editing to build a dedicated career in Cybersecurity. Having worked hands-on in every layer of digital creation gives me a distinct defensive advantage:

From Web Development

Deep understanding of code architecture, databases, and web technologies — knowing exactly how resilient, secure code must be written, validated, and hardened against vulnerabilities in the digital world.

From Graphic Design

Understanding how attackers weaponize polished visuals, brand spoofing, and deceptive layouts for phishing lures — alongside technical image-based attack vectors such as steganography, malicious payloads embedded in image formats, and transparent link overlays / clickjacking where clicking an innocuous image triggers hidden malicious actions.

From Video Editing

Recognizing how manipulated media, deceptive video edits, audio splicing, and synthetic/deepfake social engineering tactics are crafted to mislead audiences and impersonate trusted voices online.

The Hybrid Defender Advantage: Combining all of these practical experiences allows me to think like both a builder and a defender — analyzing threats from visual, human, code, and network angles to help keep people and systems safe in the digital era.
