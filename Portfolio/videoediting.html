Behind the Timeline

Why Pacing & Sound Define Every Cut

Video editing is where raw footage transforms into an emotional experience. My journey in post-production started with cutting travel films and commercial spots—discovering how a single frame cut on beat or a subtle ambient riser completely shifts how an audience feels.

"Viewers will forgive a slightly imperfect shot, but they will never stay for poor audio or sluggish pacing. Rhythm is everything."

Whether building a widescreen brand commercial in Premiere Pro and After Effects or engineering a 30-second vertical hook for social feeds, I focus on intentional narrative flow, clean color grading, and dynamic motion typography.
