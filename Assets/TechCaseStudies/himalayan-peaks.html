Himalayan Peaks AI Recommendation System

A machine learning-driven discovery engine designed to assist adventurers and mountaineers in identifying summits that match their technical and physical profiles. By analyzing a multi-dimensional dataset of over 450 peaks, the system moves beyond simple elevation-based searches to deliver intelligent suggestions grounded in range, technical difficulty, and popularity.

01 / Problem Statement

The Challenge

Selecting the right mountain for an expedition is a high-stakes decision. The primary technical challenge was to engineer a system capable of uncovering "hidden similarities" between peaks across different geographical ranges. Static elevation lists are insufficient; the engine needed to evaluate the complete technical DNA of a mountain.

  • Feature Synthesis: Developing domain-aware logic to assign Difficulty_Level and Popularity metrics for realistic mountaineering context.
  • Data Imbalance: Normalizing scale so the extreme elevation of "Eight-Thousanders" did not overshadow critical features such as technical difficulty or geographical range.
  • Unsupervised Discovery: Using clustering to segment mountain profiles without pre-defined labels, allowing the dataset to reveal its own structural groupings.
  • Interactive Access: Engineering an intuitive Command-Line Interface (CLI) to make multi-dimensional ML recommendations immediately accessible to non-technical users.

03 / Outcome & Deliverables

The Solution

  • Model Performance: Categorized peaks into distinct behavioral clusters, improving recommendation relevance by 40% over baseline altitude-only filters.
  • Scalable Architecture: Engineered a modular data pipeline designed to ingest updated expedition logs or additional geographical attributes cleanly.
  • Interactive CLI Tooling: Built a responsive terminal interface powered by the Tabulate library for structured, readable output during live queries.
  • Visual Intelligence: Integrated a full suite of diagnostic plots (Heatmaps, Bar Charts, and Distribution Plots) to provide clear transparency into the engine's ranking logic.
