Everything you need to know about working together, project timelines, and technical capabilities.

I offer end-to-end digital creative, engineering, and security services:

  • Graphic Design: Brand identity development, logo construction, vector illustrations, promotional brochures, and visual packaging.
  • Video Editing: High-retention social media reels, cinematic montages, web-series voice dubbing synchronization, and promotional ad edits using Premiere Pro, VN, and After Effects.
  • Full-Stack Web Development: Custom responsive websites, web app architecture (Python/Django, Bootstrap, MySQL), and database-driven solutions.
  • Cybersecurity: Network reconnaissance, web application vulnerability scanning, packet-level diagnostics, secure code review, and visual/media phishing defense using Nmap, OWASP ZAP, Burp Suite, and Wireshark.

Simply send a direct email to biwash.creatives@gmail.com or message me on LinkedIn with your project scope, ideal deadline, and budget. Direct communication keeps the workflow clear and eliminates form submission glitches or database overhead.

I am based in Pokhara, Nepal, but I collaborate seamlessly with clients, agencies, and businesses globally across different time zones—including markets in Nepal, India, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada—using remote workflows (Email, Slack, Zoom, GitHub).

My core technical stack includes Python and Django for backend logic, MySQL for relational database management, and Bootstrap 5 / HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript for clean, mobile-responsive user interfaces across desktops, tablets, folding phones, and mini screens.

Timeline depends on project complexity:

  • Graphic & Brand Assets: 2 – 5 business days.
  • Video Editing & Reels: 1 – 3 business days.
  • Full-Stack Web Development: 1 – 3 weeks depending on functional requirements.
  • Cybersecurity Assessments & Audits: 3 – 7 business days depending on target scope and depth.

Combining a BSc in Computing (London Metropolitan University), Level 4 & 5 Networking, and Cisco CCNA foundations with hands-on industry work lets me evaluate threats from both technical and human angles:

  • Application & Network Defense: Having built full-stack Django/MySQL systems, I know where code weaknesses hide and use Nmap, OWASP ZAP, Burp Suite, and Wireshark to inspect ports, proxy traffic, test authentication resilience, and harden web architectures.
  • Visual & Media Phishing Awareness: Experience in graphic design and video editing exposes how attackers craft convincing brand spoofs, malicious image payloads, and transparent clickable link overlays (clickjacking) to deceive users—allowing me to spot and defend against modern social-engineering lures.
