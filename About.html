My Journey

From Computing & Creative Craft to Cyber Security

My path began with a childhood fascination for how systems and cyber attacks work, evolved through formal Computing & Networking education, expanded into full-stack web development, branding, and video production, and now converges into Cyber Security.

01
Education & Early Roots Grade 8 Curiosity → BSc Computing · Cisco CCNA

Building a Foundation in Computing, Networking & Cyber Curiosity

My interest in cybersecurity started long before university—back in Grade 8, when I was deeply curious about how hacking works, how phishing lures deceive users, and the mechanics behind everyday cyber attacks. That early curiosity inspired me to understand technology from the inside out.

I went on to graduate from London Metropolitan University with a Bachelor's degree in Computing, developing a strong foundation in software development, programming, problem-solving, databases, and digital technologies. Throughout my degree, I was especially drawn to the Networking modules—gaining advanced knowledge across both Level 4 and Level 5—and during my summer break I also studied CCNA (Cisco), which gave me practical clarity in network protocols, routing, and infrastructure defense.

BSc Computing (London Met) Level 4 & 5 Networking Cisco CCNA Foundations Phishing & Attack Mechanics
02
Web Development 300+ Real Bookings Processed

Turning Ideas into Functional Experiences

I began developing responsive websites and web applications using technologies including Python, Django, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. Working on real-world projects helped me understand how thoughtful development can turn an idea into a useful digital product.

One of my key projects was a fully responsive grooming booking platform for Little Heart Pet Shop. The platform combined frontend design, backend development, database management, deployment, and booking functionality and has successfully processed 300+ bookings.

Python & Django MySQL HTML · CSS · JS · Bootstrap

06
Present Day · Career Transition Cyber Security & Holistic Digital Defense

Building a Career in Cyber Security Through Real-World Multi-Disciplinary Insight

Today, I am actively pursuing a dedicated Cyber Security course and transitioning my career into information security—combining my formal Computing & Networking (Level 4, Level 5 & Cisco CCNA) background with hands-on industry experience across Web Development, Visual Design, and Video Editing.

Having built real digital products and creative campaigns across these three fields gives me a distinct, multi-angle security perspective that goes beyond textbook theory:

Web Dev Lens

Code Strength & App Hardening

Understanding backend logic, databases, and web technologies reveals how strong, clean code must be written and secured against injection, session flaws, and infrastructure exploits.

Visual Design Lens

Phishing Lures & Transparent Image Links

Attackers weaponize polished graphics to spoof trust—hiding malicious URLs behind invisible/transparent clickable overlays (clickjacking) or weaponized image formats where clicking an ordinary-looking graphic triggers a payload.

Video Editing Lens

Media Manipulation & Social Engineering

Experience in video and audio post-production sharpens awareness of deceptive video lures, synthetic media manipulation, and malicious media-wrapper tactics used to bait unsuspecting users.

By uniting practical professional experience in Web Development, Visual Design, and Video Editing with offensive and defensive Cyber Security training, I can analyze digital risks from multiple perspectives—helping organizations and users stay resilient and safe in the modern digital era.
